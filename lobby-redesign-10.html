Concept 1 · Live floor map
Top-down venue with real-time attendee locations

Every attendee is a dot in the room they're in; occupancy pills update live; your position is always marked.

Concept 2 · Smart destination cards
Rooms that auto-rank by what's happening now

Live headcount, capacity, an auto-set status, and a recommended pick that re-sorts as the crowd moves.
