Imports

Values that don’t fit are held, not swallowed

Every import and every Odoo sync is checked against the lists above. A value that matches nothing is not written into the field and not silently invented — it lands here for a decision.

Incoming valueFieldRecordsAction

Nothing is mapped for you. No suggested match, no closest guess. The value is named, the records holding it are counted, and a person decides. The contact still imports — rejecting the whole record over one unrecognised field would lose the person along with the bad value — but the field stays empty until someone says what it should be.
This retires the silent merges. Today canonical.ts rewrites values on the way out of the database: internet and technology/web both become Data/IoT, education becomes EdTech, space tech becomes Aerospace, agnostic is dropped entirely. Those are decisions nobody made deliberately and nobody can see. Under this policy each one surfaces here instead.
Amount of capital and check size are not here. They stay numeric and keep their current scoring.

Values are stored as keys and displayed as labels, so renaming a word never orphans an answer. Removing one archives it — records already on it keep it and still match.
