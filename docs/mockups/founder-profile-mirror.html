Read-through, not a copy. The contact page already receives a company prop, so the link exists — the panel just never uses it. Falling back on read means nothing to sync, nothing to drift, and no migration.

Every mirrored value is labelled. "From founder" matters because these fields feed matching: staff need to know whether a funding stage came from the founder, from an Odoo import, or from AI inference. An unlabelled mirror would quietly make the founder's answer indistinguishable from an enrichment guess.

Four fields stay empty, honestly. Annual revenue, EBITDA, ARR and MRR aren't asked during onboarding, so there is nothing to mirror — the panel says not captured in onboarding rather than leaving a bare dash that looks like a bug. Adding them to onboarding is a separate decision.

Editing stops the mirror. Once staff type a value into the CRM field, that field is the CRM's own and no longer follows the company — otherwise a staff correction would silently revert.
