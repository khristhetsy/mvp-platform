Same component, all three roles. Green = already wired · blue = shell only today · red = own markup, needs migrating.

What "all dashboards" costs

  • Every tile needs a denominator decided. "12 investors" has no ceiling; "8 of 42 remediation" does. Where there's no sensible total the ring is a dashed presence marker, not a fake arc.
  • Six surfaces are on their own markup and have to move onto MetricCard — the two landing dashboards, the founder donut cards, marketing. The donut cards carry a click-through drawer that has to come with them.
  • Some footers need data that isn't loaded. Those ship with the denominator only; the flag stays off rather than inventing a stat.
