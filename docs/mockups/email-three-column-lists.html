What three across costs

At 600px wideColumnResult
Two columns~270pxCompany and person each fit on one line
Three columns~178pxLong names wrap to two lines — “NovaRhythm Biosciences”, “Revolution5 Global”. Rows stay aligned because cells are equal height
Showcase pitch line~178pxAbout 9 words per line. The summaries above were shortened to fit; longer ones clamp at three lines

Three across suits Presenting companies well — it is a scan, and shorter rows make a long roster look shorter. It is tighter on the Showcase, where the pitch line is the point. If a summary matters more than the density, two columns reads better there; both are built, so it stays a per-block choice.

Worth knowing

Outlook on Windows won’t stack. It ignores the media query that collapses three columns to one, so it renders three narrow cells at any width. That is why each cell holds a company and a person and nothing more — anything longer is what breaks there first. The Showcase pitch line is the part to watch.

A short last row leaves a gap. Five companies across three columns leaves one empty cell, as above. It is rendered as a blank cell rather than a stretched one, so the grid stays square.

Order within each block is still arbitrary — position is never written, so it is database order. Three columns makes that more visible than two did, because the eye reads across rows. Alphabetical by company would settle it.
