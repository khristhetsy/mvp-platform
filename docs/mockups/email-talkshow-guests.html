The same free-text trap as before. Because role_label is a string, the email has to match “Guest CEO” case- and spacing-insensitively, exactly as the Founder Showcase grouping does. Anything attached to the session that isn’t a recognised role renders under its own label rather than being hidden — an unmatched role should still show the person, not drop them.

Worth knowing

More than one of each is fine. Two investors on the panel render as two rows; the strip is a list, not a pair of slots. Guest CEOs come first, then investors, then anyone else.

The abstract should stop describing the format once the names are there — “a candid conversation with a CEO” reads oddly directly above the CEO’s name. That’s copy on the session record, editable in the block editor, not something this changes for you.

It applies to the booklet too, which has the same gap — presenters carry a session_id the booklet also ignores. Same fix, different renderer; say if you want both.
