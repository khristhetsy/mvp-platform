Compliance

Controls based on the issues the CNIL cited in its Kaspr decision: data users had hidden, long retention, missing notice, and vague answers to access requests. Confirm settings with counsel.

Collection rules

Only use domains and public business data, never hidden profile fields
Require a lawful basis on every lookup
Record the exact source of each record
Block personal email domains (gmail, outlook)

Retention

Do not reset the clock when a record is updated

Kaspr was cited for restarting a five year clock on each update.
