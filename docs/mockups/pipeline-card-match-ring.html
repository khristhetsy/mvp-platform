3. Three variants — pick one

A · Ring with number inside recommended

Exact figure kept, plus the arc. 44px fits the card without crowding the name. Matches the CRR ring on the public profile, so the two read as one language.

B · Ring only, number on hover

Cleanest, smallest. But a match score is a number people want to read, and hover does not exist on touch.

C · Ring + label beneath

match
match

Explicit, but the word repeats on every card and the cards are already tight.

Sizes. 44px on the kanban card, 40px if the card gets busier, 28px in the table row where the existing MatchBar lives — that one can stay a bar if you prefer, since a table already lines numbers up for you.

CaseShowsWhy
match_score is nullempty grey track, “—” insideManually added investors have no score. An empty ring says “not scored”; a 0% ring would say “scored zero”, which is a different and wrong claim.
Score of 0a 1% sliver, greyGenuinely scored and genuinely poor. The sliver distinguishes it from null.
Score of 100full ringThe arc closes. Several of your current cards sit here.
Screen readersrole="img" + aria-label="72% match"The ring is decorative on its own; the label carries the value.
Printing / high contrastnumber stays insideVariant A survives a black-and-white print; variant B does not.
Worth noticing while you are here. Four of the nine cards in your screenshot read 100% match. A match score that saturates at 100 for most of a list is the same ceiling problem the CRR factors have — the ring will make it obvious at a glance, which is useful, but the fix is in the matcher rather than the card.
