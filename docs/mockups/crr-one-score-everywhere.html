The 87 is a count of document types, dressed as a score. diligence_reports.readiness_score is null on the AI path, so the fallback fires: max(55, 90 − missing×6). Nothing in that chain touches a factor, a dimension, or a weight — so re-scoring, re-weighting, and everything you tuned today cannot move it.
And the gates disagree. The founder pages unlock outreach at 70 (OUTREACH_THRESHOLD in investable-score.ts and again, hard-coded, in founder/deploy/page.tsx). The engine writes outreach_unlocked at 65 on the score row. Your page says “Investor outreach is unlocked” on the strength of 87 ≥ 70, while the engine’s own flag — the one founder-crm/outreach-readiness.ts actually reads — says otherwise at 34. That is a correctness bug, not a naming one.

This is the whole decision. Founders who were told they were outreach-ready will be told they are not. If any of them have been reached out to on that basis, or have paid on that basis, that needs handling before the switch — not after.

Three ways to land it

OptionWhat happens
Hard cutSwitch every surface at once. Honest and simple. Every founder sees their number fall by ~50 points with no warning.
Grandfather the gate recShow the engine score everywhere immediately, but anyone whose outreach is unlocked today stays unlocked. New unlocks use the engine. Nobody loses access they already had.
StagedShow both for a period — “CRR 34 · preparation 87” — then retire the old one. Honest, but two numbers on screen is the confusion you are trying to end.
Grandfathering is one boolean on the company, set once at switch-over for anyone currently unlocked. The score changes for everyone; access changes for nobody until they earn it.
Before any of this: the engine may be too harsh, not the old number too kind. Nobody in 22 companies reaches 40. That is the ceiling/floor problem from the discrimination work — 63 of 100 points sit in factors more than half your companies score zero on. Switching the platform to a rubric that fails everyone is worse than the inconsistency it fixes. Tune the Pre-seed/Seed points and re-score first, look at the distribution, then switch.
