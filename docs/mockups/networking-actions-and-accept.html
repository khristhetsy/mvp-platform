When they chose the lounge or messaging instead, that box says so and the link goes to the lounge or the thread. The email is the same shape either way.

Worth knowing

“Talk now” is the only one of the four that exists, and it has a catch. The Meet is created from the clicker’s own connected Google account. If neither has connected Google — and most attendees haven’t — the button fails. Today that failure happens after the accept, which is the worst possible moment.

So the fix is that the link comes from us, not from them. The event already mints rooms for its sessions; introductions should use the same path, so neither party needs a Google account at all. That is the part of “Book a time” that actually costs work.

A booked slot can collide with the event itself. An introduction at 1:30 PM puts both of them in a side call during the talk show. The slot picker should offer times inside the event but outside a session either of them is presenting in, and say plainly when a slot overlaps something on the agenda.

Accepting reveals identity both ways. The investor accepts and the founder learns the investor’s firm — but the investor also becomes visible to the founder, having previously seen only a name. That is the right trade, and the invitation says so; it is worth making sure the decline path stays silent, because a declined introduction the founder can see is worse than no introduction.
