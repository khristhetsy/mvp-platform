The three tiers, and how the requirement is flagged in the Founder's Marketing menu · mockup

Settled A low CRR does not stop the founder advancing to Stage 3. It stops exactly two things once they are there: investor match requests and automated introductions. No new concept, no new status — the existing Capital Readiness Rating and Recommendations are the requirement and the guidance.

TierActionGate todayChange
1 Founder's own list
manual email, events, browsing matches and the Matching Center		 None Nothing. Confirming it stays open at any CRR is the point — this is where a founder below the requirement does the work that raises it. Browsing matches belongs here; pressing Request introduction does not.
2 Automated Outreach CRR ≥ 65 Already correct in outreach-readiness.ts. The change is visibility — the Marketing row shows the reason up front instead of the founder discovering it at the point of sending.
3 Brokered Introduction
the investor match request		 No CRR gate The real gap. /api/founder/matching/intro gates on plan (canBrokerIntros), on the monthly cap, and on email dispatch — but never on CRR. A founder at 32 on Professional can request an introduction today. Three surfaces call it: founder/matching, founder/matches, investor-pipeline/[id]. The gate goes in the route so all three are covered, and the button is disabled with the reason on each.
Everything in the menu already exists. The CRR row uses the same MirrorItem shape, the same status chips, the same drawer with problem / what's missing / how to solve, and the same ItemDiagnosis.ring that Preparation's CRR row already draws. The new code is one entry in the deploy menu definition, a condition reading crr.outreachUnlocked, and the missing gate on the intro route.

Why repeat the CRR row in Marketing when Preparation has it. The requirement does not stop applying when the founder advances. Today a company sits in Marketing at CRR 32 and nothing on the Marketing tab says so — the number lives one tab back, and the two items that depend on it fail silently at the point of use. The row puts the requirement where the work is.

Held means held. Both tier 2 and tier 3 are hard stops below 65 — the founder is in Stage 3, can see everything, and can work their own list, but iCapOS does not send on their behalf and does not introduce them until the rating clears. The stage moves; the endorsement does not.
