2 · The founder's intro note stops at the admin

The founder writes a note when requesting an introduction, and it is stored correctly — intro_requests.message. Both admin surfaces render it: AdminIntroQueue shows “Founder's intro note”, and so does ProspectIntrosClient.

The investor never sees it. InvestorFacilitatedIntrosPanel selects id, company_id, facilitated_at, created_at, companies(…) — message is not in the query and not on the screen. The founder writes the one thing that would make the introduction land, and it is dropped at the last step.

Same component the two admin surfaces already use, pointed at the same column. The only change is adding message to the select and rendering it — and showing nothing at all when the founder left the note blank, rather than the default placeholder string.

Why one gate rather than three. Right now a founder can be blocked from Outreach at CRR 64 and still request an Introduction at CRR 12, having already advanced to Marketing on document count alone. Three doors, three rules, and the number on the card explains none of them. Using crr.outreachUnlocked everywhere means the 33-short figure on the CRR card is the literal, only reason any of the three is shut.

This will block companies that are already through. Enforcing the CRR on Stage 3 is retroactive — companies that advanced on the old document rule may sit below 65 today, like the one in your screenshot. Worth deciding whether the gate applies to advancing only (already-advanced companies keep Marketing), or to acting too (they keep the stage but lose Introduction and Outreach until the score catches up). The first is kinder; the second is the one that actually protects the firm's name.

One thing I did not verify. Whether a staff override should bypass the gate — an admin approving a company they know is ready despite the score. The CRR engine already supports an override on the score itself, which may be the cleaner lever than a second bypass.
