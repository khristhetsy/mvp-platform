The three levers, and only three

Background

tinted band plain

The feature breaks the margins and sits on a pale navy band. The Showcase stays on white, so the eye lands on the band first.

Scale

20 / 13.4 13 / 11.6

Heading and company name both step down for the Showcase. A third smaller is enough to read as secondary without looking like an afterthought.

Card weight

card + rule hairline cells

Separated cards with a navy top rule and a soft shadow, against flat cells divided by hairlines.

No colour was added and nothing was made bigger than the hero — the register button is still the loudest thing in the email, which is the point of sending it.

Worth knowing

This demotes the block with the most persuasive content. The Showcase carries the pitch lines — the part that tells an investor why to come — and it is now the quietest thing on the page. If the Showcase is the draw and Presenting companies is the credit, this is backwards; say so and I’ll swap the weighting rather than the order.

The tinted band breaks the body margins. That needs a full-width table row in the email, not padding, or Outlook on Windows will leave a white gutter down both sides. Worth knowing because it is the one part of this that isn’t just a style value.

Emphasis is per block, not per email. A reminder that leads with the Showcase only needs the two levers reversed — so this is a setting on the block, not a rewrite.
