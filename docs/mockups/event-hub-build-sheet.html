Four things only you can answer

1 · Which session gets the tint?

Auditorium now leads, but the Talk Show is the one with named guests. The featured treatment can follow the order or follow the guests.

→ Order (Auditorium tinted)  ·  Guests (Talk Show tinted, second)

2 · Who verifies a badge?

“Investor” is self-selected at registration and about to appear publicly, where it reads as our endorsement.

→ Staff can correct before it shows  ·  Publish as chosen  ·  No badge on the public page, only in the email

3 · Do sessions move with the event date?

Each session holds its own time, so moving the event leaves all four behind. The dialog reports it either way.

→ Offer “shift sessions too” as a tick  ·  Always shift  ·  Never, just warn

4 · Is the direction right?

I read it as: invitation goes to the investor, about the founder; the follow-up chases the investor on the founder’s behalf.

→ Correct  ·  Flip it

Worth knowing

Las Vegas is on the 22nd — today. Phase 1 is the only part worth having before it, and the end-session confirmation is the one that matters most during a live event.

Networking Matching is the one to be slow about. It is the only piece that turns attendee-to-attendee requests into introductions sent by us, and the score behind it is shared sectors ×2 plus 3. Running the 22nd on the existing flow costs nothing and tells you whether people want brokered introductions at all.
