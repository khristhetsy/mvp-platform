Why only Financial model works

It is the only one of the nine that lets the acting session through the gate. Its page body branches on acting — when staff are acting as the founder it renders the content directly; otherwise it wraps in FounderJourneyGate. The other eight wrap in that gate unconditionally.

So “Open as founder” does start the session correctly on all nine — the founder page then refuses to render, and you land on the stage-gate screen instead of the founder's Cap table or Pitch deck. The button is right. The eight pages are not.

I checked all nine: acting ? … : <Gate> appears in financial-model only. That is the whole difference.

Why bypassing the gate is right here, and is not a hole. The journey gate exists to stop a founder skipping ahead of their own stage. A staff member acting on their behalf is doing the opposite job — looking at what the founder has, usually to help them past that very stage. The authorization that matters already ran: resolveActingFounderScope re-proves on every request that the caller is staff, still holds act_on_behalf, and started this session themselves. The gate is a product rule, not a security boundary.

The plan gate is a different question. Some of these pages also sit behind the founder's subscription plan (the padlocks in your sidebar). Bypassing a stage gate for staff is clearly right; bypassing a paid-plan gate is a decision — it lets staff see a surface the founder has not bought. Financial model currently bypasses both, because the branch is around the whole body. Worth saying which you want before this is built.
