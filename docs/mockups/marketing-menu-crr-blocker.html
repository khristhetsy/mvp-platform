Two things are wrong here, and they share one cause.
  • There is no CRR row. The number that gates this whole stage appears nowhere in the menu.
  • Automated outreach says “Open” while its own detail line says “blocked — 17 points to the gate”. The chip and the sentence next to it contradict each other.
The cause: in STAGE_MENU.deploy only Investor CRM carries a condition. Every other row falls through to status: "todo" — rendered as “Open” — no matter what the diagnosis found. That is also why the header reads 0 of 1 measured · 5 not tracked and why the blocking count is 1 when two things are genuinely blocked.

Note also the chip strip: Readiness 78 is the Preparation completion figure. The CRR is 48 — that is what “17 points to the gate” of 65 means. Two numbers, thirty points apart, and only the one that gates nothing is on display.

What this takes
FileChange
founder-journey/types.ts StageConditions gains crrScore: number | null and crrQualified: boolean.
founder-journey/evaluate.ts One crrFor(companyId) call; crrQualified = crr.outreachUnlocked. Reads the engine's own gate — never a hard-coded 65.
admin/stage-menu-mirror.ts Add "crrQualified" to ConditionKey. One new deploy entry for the CRR row, and condition: "crrQualified" added to Automated outreach, Investor matches and Matching Center. That is what turns “0 of 1 measured · 5 not tracked” into “0 of 3 measured” and the blocking count from 1 to 3 — doneCount, measuredCount and unmet already derive from condition, so no counting logic changes.
admin/stage-diagnosis.ts Already has crrDiagnosis and already keys it by href. Point the new row's href at it — the drawer above is that function's existing output.
stage-menu-mirror.ts qualify list The row labelled Capital Readiness Rating in Preparation reads readinessQualified — the document count. Repoint it to crrQualified in the same pass; that is the mismatch left commented in the last change.
Why a row rather than just fixing the chip. Fixing Automated outreach alone would show blocked without showing by what — staff would still have to open the row to learn the number. The CRR row puts the requirement, the gap and the ordered fixes at the top of the stage that depends on them, and the two held rows then just point at it.

Reminder of what this does not change. The founder still reaches Stage 3 at any CRR — canRequestApproval is untouched. Marketplace and Present at event stay open. Only the two things that go out under the iCapOS name are held.
