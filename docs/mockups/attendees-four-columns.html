Four columns

Aligned columns, one badge per group heading instead of 101 times, and it scans alphabetically down each column.

The badge is carried by the avatar colour — blue for investors, violet for founders — and named once in the group heading. Long names ellipsis rather than wrap, so rows stay aligned. Four columns become three, two and one as the page narrows.
