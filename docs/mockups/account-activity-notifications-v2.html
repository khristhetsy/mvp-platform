Quiet hours, pause all and critical override already exist at /admin/sales/settings/notifications and apply here unchanged. This adds four folded blocks to that page rather than a second settings screen.
Why fold by stage rather than by category. The thirteen OPERATIONAL_EVENT_CATEGORIES are how the table is indexed, not how you work. You work a company through four stages, and the same event means something different depending on where the founder is — a replaced pitch deck in Preparation is progress; the same upload in Closing, after investors have read the old one, is a problem. Stage is the axis that carries the judgement.

Assignment is per stage, not per event. Twenty-one classes × five staff is a grid nobody maintains. Four stage owners is a thing you can actually keep current, and it matches how the work is already divided. Three classes override it (+ Compliance, + CEO) because they aren't stage work — they're firm risk.

Two things the stage framing exposes that v1 missed. Outreach launched below the gate and offering type changed after an SPV opened are the events most worth a phone call, and neither is a single field change — each is a founder action read against the stage it happened in. That check has to live in the emitter, not in the notification layer.
