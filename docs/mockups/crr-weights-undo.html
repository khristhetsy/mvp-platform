Three kinds of undo, because they are three different problems. Nothing built. What exists today: Reset (throw away all unsaved edits), Reset to code defaults, and Revert in History. What is missing is stepwise undo while typing, a one-click undo on a single number, and a way to take back a save you have just made.

1. Undo / Redo while editing — the one you asked for

A stack of drafts held in the browser. Every change to a factor point, a band or a floor pushes a step. The label says what it would undo, so you are never guessing.

Typing a number counts as one step, not six. Edits to the same input inside two seconds collapse into a single undo step, so pressing ⌘Z after typing “14” takes you back to 30 rather than to “1”.

2. Undo a save — the one that actually matters

Saving appends a version and, if you ticked re-score, rewrites every company’s score. This is the case where an undo is worth real money.

Immediately after saving

✓ Saved crr-profiles-v2 · Pre-seed Pitch 30 → 14, Team 30 → 24 · 22 companies re-scored, average 17 → 21

Stays until you leave the tab. After that, History ’s Revert does the same job for any version, forever.

What Undo this save does

StepActionDetail
1Copies crr-profiles-v1 forwardSaves it as v3 with the reason “Undo of v2” and makes it active. v1 and v2 are both left intact.
2Re-scores the 22 companiesArithmetic over stored factor scores — no AI, no cost, a few seconds. Scores land back exactly where they were.
3Records itHistory shows v3 “Undo of v2”, and the audit log carries who and when.

And forward again — Redo the save

Undoing leaves its own banner. Changed your mind twice? Redo puts v2’s numbers back as v4 and re-scores again.

↶ Undone. crr-profiles-v3 is active with v1’s numbers · 22 companies re-scored back, average 21 → 17

The version chain, both directions

v1seeded from code
→
v2Pitch 30 → 14 your save
→
v3undo of v2 = v1’s numbers
→
v4redo of v2 = v2’s numbers

Nothing is deleted. Undo and redo both move the chain forward; the active flag moves with them. History reads as a story rather than a set of holes.

It undoes forward, not backward — and that is deliberate. The version list and company_readiness_scores are append-only: they are the audit trail for why a company scored what it scored on a given day. So an undo adds a corrective version rather than deleting the mistake. A company’s score timeline will show the spike and the return, which is the truth of what happened.

If you would rather a mistaken save vanish entirely, that is a different feature — a hard delete of a version and its score rows — and I would argue against it for a number investors read.
Most of this already exists. Revert in History does exactly steps 1–3 today. The new part is the banner: it pre-fills the target version and the reason, so undoing is one click rather than finding the right row.

4. What it costs

PieceCostNotes
Undo / Redo stacksmallTwo arrays of draft snapshots in the panel’s state, plus a keydown handler and the two-second coalescing. No server, no migration.
↺ per rowtinyThe panel already compares each input to the active version to colour it — the button reuses that comparison.
Undo / Redo a savesmallRemember two version ids — the one active before the save, and the one you just undid — then call the existing revert action with whichever direction you pressed. The API needs nothing new.
Edit-history listoptionalNice for confidence, not required. Say if you want it or just the two buttons.
