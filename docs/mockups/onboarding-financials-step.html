Onboarding — the four missing fields

Founder wizard gains a step 8; the answers surface on the founder profile, the CRM panel and the investor view.

What's actually missing

The wizard is TOTAL = 7 steps and already captures twelve of the CRM panel's nineteen fields — seeking_investor_types, funding_stage, operating_stage, business_entity, annual_ebitda, management_team and the rest.

Only four have no source at all: Annual revenue size, ARR, MRR and Five key highlights. (revenue_stage exists but it's a band like "Pre-revenue", not a figure — mapping it to "Annual revenue size" would be wrong.)

These four are also the ones the CRR engine's Traction and Revenue factors need. Today those factors score zero for want of evidence, not performance.

Three decisions worth your eye.

1 · Revenue is a band, not a figure. Investors filter by range, and a band is far likelier to be answered honestly than an exact number. It also matches revenue_stage, which already exists — so the two need reconciling rather than sitting side by side meaning almost the same thing.

2 · The step is skippable. Making ARR mandatory would push pre-revenue founders to invent one, and the CRR engine would then score fiction. "Skip for now" keeps the field empty and honest — the readiness drawer already tells them what it costs them in points.

3 · Not everything is investor-facing. EBITDA and the team summary are internal; revenue band and highlights are public on the one-pager. The step states this up front rather than surprising them later, and the columns need a visibility rule in code, not just a label here.

Also: these columns went in with migration 20260803002 but db:types hasn't been run since, so the wizard and route reach them through a Record<string, unknown> cast. Adding four more the same way works, but regenerating the types would remove the casts everywhere.
