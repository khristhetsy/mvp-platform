Why it belongs on this page rather than a new one. The founder already comes here to think about events, the plan tiers are already explained here, and an accepted invitation becomes a speaker_application with status: approved — the same row an application produces, just created from the other direction. A separate "My invitations" page would split one subject across two places.

A founder with an account never needs the emailed token link. They can click it, but it should recognise their session and land them here. The tokenless page is for exhibitors and outside speakers with no iCapOS account.
The decision this forces: does an invitation override the plan gate? present-tiers.ts gives Free founders no presenting at all, and Basic only Spotlight. So:
  • A Free founder you invite — today the page would show them an upgrade wall. Do they present because you asked them to, or do they have to pay first?
  • A Basic founder invited to a full stage slot — that is the Professional tier. Does the invitation grant it for this one event?
My read: an invitation should override the gate for that event only, and the page should say so plainly — "Invited by iCFO — no plan upgrade needed for this event". You invited them; making them pay to accept is a strange conversation, and staff inviting someone is already a deliberate act. But it is your call, and it changes what PresentAtEventClient renders for a Free founder.
