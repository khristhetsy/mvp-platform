5. What this costs to build, honestly

Straightforward

A plan_prices table seeded from today’s numbers; one usePricing() read; the three stale surfaces rewired; the two duplicate catalogues (plans.ts and billing/pricing.ts) collapsed into one; the $800 retyped in two places reading the constant that already exists.

Needs care

The i18n CTA keys are named founder_basic_499_mo — the price is in the key, so both language files change. And the public pricing page is a third hand-written copy that has to start reading the catalogue.

Can’t be automated

LemonSqueezy prices are immutable and not editable through their API. The editor can read the variant price and warn when it disagrees, but changing what is actually charged stays a job in their dashboard.

Deliberately out of scope

Forecast ARPU (an assumption you tune per scenario, flagged not overwritten), already-published page-builder pages (free text), and historical MRR (must keep reporting what was billed).
