Email is the first item, as on Opportunities. It opens the same MassEmailComposer the admin pages use, with the selection passed as ids. The composer already understands two sources (contacts, opportunities); founder lists add their own — a small, typed addition rather than a new component.
The one thing to decide before this is built. A founder emailing investors directly from the platform is a different thing from staff doing it: it touches the “iCFO is not a broker-dealer, introductions are facilitated” line the public profile already carries. Options, cheapest first —
  • Email only contacts the founder already owns (their own CRM, people who contacted them). Investors from the match list get “Request intro” instead, which routes through iCapOS. Safest, and it matches how the product is described today.
  • Email anyone, plan-gated — Professional and above, with a monthly cap, reusing the outreach caps that already exist.
  • Email anyone, uncapped. I would not; it turns the marketplace into a cold-outreach list.

Nothing new to build underneath. OdooSearchBar, SelectionBar, SelectionAction and MassEmailComposer all exist and are in use. This is wiring them into founder pages, giving each one its filter/group lists, and pointing the bulk actions at founder-scoped endpoints.

Suggested order: the toolbar and search first across all eight pages — it is the visible half and carries no policy questions. Then selection + Actions, starting with Matches and Pipeline. Email last, once you have decided which of the three options in section 3 you want.
