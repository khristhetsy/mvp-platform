Why the flat numbers don't read

  • The two that matter most are indistinguishable from the two that don't.

Each ring needs a denominator, and three of the five didn't have one. Completion and CRR are already out of 100. Remediation becomes 8 open of 42 tracked and compliance 1 open of 12 — both then read as progress rather than a bare count, and the ring fills green as they close.

Investor interests has no ceiling at all, so it gets a dashed empty ring rather than a fake one — the honest shape for "nothing recorded yet". Its supporting line carries the reason: outreach is locked until CRR 65, which is the same number the ring beside it is 20 short of.

The CRR ring marks the gate. A red tick at 65 turns "45" from a number into a distance, which is the only thing a reviewer needs from this card.
