Investor Relations playbook

Johnny Rivera · updated Sep 16 · 14 versions

The playbook covers the four-to-six month engagement: onboarding, weekly outreach, meeting prep, and founder reporting. Each section links to the live screen it describes.

Weekly outreach cadence

Monday: run matching on the week's task (→ IR Hub · Tasks). Tuesday–Thursday: intros and calls, logged on the Share Project record. Friday: clear blockers, prep next week. Targets come from Goals.

Linked records
IR project · Doyle OrganicsTask · Week 3 outreachTemplate · Intro email

Founder report approval

Draft the AI summary, edit, approve, then send. Nothing goes out unapproved. Firm names appear only once a meeting is booked.
