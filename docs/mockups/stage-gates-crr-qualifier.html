All 3 Preparation gates met. Ready to request approval — 3 later gates shown greyed for context.

A gate that isn't due yet is not a failure. The panel today marks every unmet gate with a red ✕ and rolls it into the pending count — so a company in Preparation is shown failing “Deal room created” and “Investor interest logged”, neither of which it could possibly have. Putting CRR in as a sixth red ✕ would make that worse: the founder would be marked short of a rating before the stage that uses it.

So each gate carries the stage it belongs to. Gates for the current stage score ✓ / ✕ and count as pending. Gates for a later stage render grey with ◷ Due in {stage}, are still clickable for the preview, and are excluded from the count. Nothing is hidden — a staff member can see CRR 32 from Preparation and start the conversation early. It just isn't called a failure until it is one.

Why the rename is right, not cosmetic. That gate has never measured readiness — it reads computeReadinessScore(uploadedTypeCodes), which is a count of document types present. Calling it “Readiness qualified” next to a real Capital Readiness Rating would give the panel two numbers claiming the same name. Preparation complete says what it measures: how much of the Preparation stage is filled in. CRR says the other thing: how good what's filled in actually is. It also sits naturally beside Onboarding complete — same shape, same kind of claim, one stage on.

Four places carry the label, not two. This panel and the reminder emails say “Readiness qualified”; founder/journey/page.tsx and StageProgressCard.tsx say “Completion ≥ 75%”. All four become Preparation complete, so the founder and the staff member are reading the same words for the same gate.

The CRR gate is visible, not blocking. It shows red and counts in “3 pending”, but canRequestApproval is not touched — a low CRR never stops the founder reaching Stage 3. What it stops is two things once they are there:

1 · Investor match request — /api/founder/matching/intro. Today it gates on plan, monthly cap and email dispatch, but never on CRR, so a founder at 32 can request a brokered introduction. Browsing the matches stays open; the Request introduction button is what goes disabled, with the reason and the points short on it.

2 · Automated introduction — Automated Outreach, already correct at CRR ≥ 65 in outreach-readiness.ts.

So the sixth gate on this panel is information for the staff member: it explains why those two controls are dark on the founder's Marketing menu. The stage moves; the endorsement does not.

And it only turns red in Marketing. In Preparation it reads ◷ Due in Marketing — visible, clickable, outside the pending count. Nothing about the score is hidden from staff; it just isn't held against the founder before the stage that requires it.
