How event matching works

Short version: there are two things called matching on this platform, they are not the same engine, and the one that runs at an event is deliberately simple.

First — which matching?

Event networking

lib/icfo-events/networking.ts

  • Attendee ↔ attendee, inside one event
  • Opt-in, both directions
  • Score: shared sectors, and that’s nearly it
  • Returns names only
  • Ends in a connection request, then a Google Meet
NOT

Investor matching

lib/matching/  ·  Matching Center

  • Founder ↔ investor, platform-wide
  • 9 weighted factors, CRR-gated, staff-reviewed
  • State machine, cron pass, anonymised cards
  • Introductions, not connections
  • No awareness of events at all
One bridge between them. When someone registers for an event, the sectors they declare on the form are copied into a per-event networking opt-in. Nothing else crosses — no match score, no investor profile, no event_id on any match record. lib/icfo-events/registration-intake.ts

The flow, end to end

1
They register, and declare sectors

Investors answer sectors; founders answer sector. Registration intake turns those answers into a networking opt-in scoped to this event only — it never writes to the core profile.

registration-intake.ts → upsertOptin(eventId, profileId, true, interests)

2
Or they opt in by hand on the event page

A checkbox plus an interests picker. Sponsors and service providers don’t get opted in at all — they route to event_leads instead.

Gate · no opt-in, no suggestions — in either direction

components/events/NetworkingOptIn.tsx → networking_optins

3
Suggestions are scored on the spot

Every other opted-in attendee at that event is scored against them, anything scoring zero is dropped, and the top 10 come back. No stored match rows, no background pass — it is computed each time the page loads.

listSuggestions(supabase, eventId, profileId, limit = 10)

4
A connection request, then an answer

Requested → accepted or declined. Unique per (event, from, to), and you can’t request yourself. Accepting is worth 15 points and earns the Connector badge.

networking_connections · status requested | accepted | declined

5
Only then, a call

A Google Meet created from the requester’s own connected Google account, starting now, running an hour. It is a “talk now” button, not a bookable slot — there is no meeting-scheduler for events.

Gate · accepted connections only

api/events/networking/call

The score, in full

score = shared.length * 2
      + complementaryBonus

// complementaryBonus = 3
// when one is a founder and
// the other an investor

// keep score > 0, sort desc, take 10
SituationScore
Investor & founder, 3 sectors in common9
Investor & founder, 1 sector in common5
Two founders, 2 sectors in common4
Investor & founder, nothing in common3
Two investors, nothing in common0 — dropped

That is the entire model. No stage, no cheque size, no geography, no CRR, no readiness — none of the nine factors the investor engine uses.

What an attendee actually sees

Suggested for you
AMAna MoralesConnect
DRDaniel ReyesConnect
SKSofia KimConnect

Names, nothing else — no company, no score, no reason. That is the opt-in trust model: you consented to being suggested, not to having your profile handed out. Company and context only appear once the other person accepts.

Things worth knowing before you rely on it

Honest limits

  • Auto opt-in fails silently. Registration intake wraps the opt-in in a swallowed catch — if it fails, the attendee is simply absent from everyone’s suggestions and nobody is told.
  • Sector vocabularies must agree. The match is a plain string intersection. The investor engine has a synonym table (AI/ML ↔ Technology ↔ SaaS); event networking does not, so “FinTech” and “Financial Technology” score zero against each other.
  • Admin can’t see any of it. Event analytics shows a count of opt-ins and nothing more — no view of who was suggested to whom, or which connections were made.
  • A founder’s investor matches and their event suggestions are unrelated. A strong platform match who is in the room will not be surfaced at the event unless they separately opted in, and even then only on shared sectors.
