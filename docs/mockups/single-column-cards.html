What changes, and where

  • Four grid helpers drive nearly every card layout — MetricGrid (4-up, 5 files), MetricGridWide (3-up), ContentGrid (2–3 up, 3 files), plus one inline grid gap-6 xl:grid-cols-2 in the company workspace. Change those and the whole platform follows.
  • A full-width card has room the tiles never had. The ring, the number and the supporting sentence fit on one line, so "8" becomes "8 open of 42 · 2 high priority · 4 closed this week" without wrapping.
  • The honest cost: five stacked cards are taller than one row of five, so the sections below get pushed down. Mitigated by making the rows compact — roughly 62px each rather than a 140px tile.

One change, four helpers. MetricGrid and MetricGridWide become a vertical stack, ContentGrid defaults to columns={1}, and the inline xl:grid-cols-2 in the workspace drops to one. Every module that uses them follows without edits.

The card itself has to change shape too, or you get a 140px-tall tile stretched across 1000px of nothing. The metric becomes a row: ring, then label and value, then the supporting sentence filling the space, then the drill-down link right-aligned. That is what buys the extra detail — "14 open of 18 tracked · 4 high priority · oldest open 46 days" doesn't fit in a quarter-width tile.

Worth deciding: whether this applies on every breakpoint or only below a width. Full width on a 27-inch monitor gives a very long line; capping the row content at ~900px keeps it readable while the card stays full width.
