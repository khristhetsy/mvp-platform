Worth knowing

Uniqueness is per event, not global. Two different events may both want “Issue 2026”; two booklets for the same event may not share a name. That is the collision you actually hit.

Renaming stays possible. The name is editable in the builder afterwards, and the same check applies there — so you can’t rename one booklet into another’s name.

The two drafts already there aren’t cleaned up by this. They are real rows; delete whichever you don’t want with the Delete button. Nothing here removes data on your behalf.
