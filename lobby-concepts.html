iCapOS Event Lobby — 5 interactive concepts

Each concept adds live presence ("who's where") and automation to the lobby. Counts, dots, and feeds update on their own — hover and click to explore.

◔ Concept 1 · Live floor map
Top-down venue with real-time attendee locations

Every attendee is a dot inside the room they're in. Occupancy pills update live, and your position is always marked. Click a room to jump in.

▤ Concept 2 · Smart destination cards
Auto-ranked rooms with live headcount and status

Rooms sort themselves by what's happening now. Each card shows who's inside, capacity, and a status the system sets automatically — with a recommended pick.

◈ Concept 3 · Enhanced immersive lobby
Your current lobby, plus live counts and presence

Keeps the room feel you have now, but each doorway shows a live count badge, lifts on hover, and the avatars below are real attendees milling in the lobby.
