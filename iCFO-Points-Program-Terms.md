## 1. What iCFO Points are

iCFO Points ("Points") are a **loyalty and engagement reward**. They are earned by participating in iCFO events and community activities and may be redeemed for eligible iCFO products and services described below.

Points are an internal rewards unit. **They are not money, currency, legal tender, a stored-value instrument, a gift card, a security, or a deposit. Points have no cash value and are never redeemable for cash.** Points represent a limited, personal, revocable license to access eligible iCFO services — nothing more.

## 2. No cash value

Points:

- have **no cash value** and cannot be exchanged, redeemed, or refunded for cash;
- cannot be sold, transferred, assigned, gifted, combined across accounts, or used as collateral;
- confer no ownership, equity, interest, or financial claim in iCFO, any issuer, any offering, or any third party;
- are not insured by any government agency and are not a bank or financial-institution product.

Points are available to registered iCFO users in good standing who accept these terms. We may exclude users, roles, or jurisdictions where the program is restricted or impractical. One rewards balance per verified account; duplicate or fraudulent accounts are ineligible.
